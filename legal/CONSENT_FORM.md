## Introduction
This document explains what data the SASOK platform ("Platform") collects, how it is used, and what your rights are. Please read it carefully. By using SASOK, you agree to these terms.

**The Platform's Goal:** SASOK is a symbiotic cognitive-emotional platform designed for digital self-reflection and the creation of an "Emotional ID." It is intended for scientific, therapeutic, and cultural-artistic purposes.

---

## Part 1: Data Collection & Processing

To provide its services, the Platform needs to collect and process several types of data. You have control over what you share.

### 1.1. Visual Data (Video Stream)
*   **What we collect:** Real-time video stream from your camera. Our AI models analyze facial expressions (based on 68 facial landmarks), head position, and eye movements.
*   **How we use it:** To interpret non-verbal cues and understand your emotional state (e.g., joy, sadness, surprise). This helps create a more accurate reflection.
*   **Storage:** Raw video is processed in real-time and is **not** stored by default. Only the derived anonymous emotional vectors are saved. You may optionally consent to temporary, encrypted storage of video clips for research purposes.
*   **Your Control:** You can disable camera access at any time.

[ ] **I consent** to the collection and processing of my **Visual Data** for real-time emotional analysis as described above.

### 1.2. Audial Data (Audio Stream)
*   **What we collect:** Real-time audio from your microphone. Our AI models analyze intonation, pitch, volume, and speech patterns. We do **not** transcribe or store the content of your speech unless you explicitly use a dictation feature.
*   **How we use it:** To detect emotional tone (e.g., excitement, calmness, stress) from your voice.
*   **Storage:** Raw audio is processed in real-time and is **not** stored. Only the derived anonymous emotional vectors are saved.
*   **Your Control:** You can mute your microphone at any time.

[ ] **I consent** to the collection and processing of my **Audial Data** for real-time emotional analysis as described above.

### 1.3. Biometric Data (Wearable Sensors)
*   **What we collect:** Data from connected wearable devices, such as Heart Rate (HR), Heart Rate Variability (HRV), and Galvanic Skin Response (GSR).
*   **How we use it:** To measure physiological arousal and stress levels, providing a deeper layer of insight into your emotional state.
*   **Storage:** Time-series biometric data is stored in an encrypted, pseudonymized format, linked only to your internal user ID.
*   **Your Control:** You can disconnect your wearable device at any time.

[ ] **I consent** to the collection and processing of my **Biometric Data** from connected devices.

### 1.4. Behavioral Data
*   **What we collect:** Interaction patterns with the Platform, such as typing speed, click patterns, and response times.
*   **How we use it:** To infer your cognitive state (e.g., focus, hesitation, distraction).
*   **Storage:** This data is stored as pseudonymized logs.

[ ] **I consent** to the collection and processing of my **Behavioral Data** to improve the Platform's responsiveness.
